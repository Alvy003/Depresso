Welcome To Our Website

Welcome to our platform, a dedicated space designed to help individuals better understand their mental well-being. We believe that awareness is the first step toward improvement, and our goal is to provide an accessible, user-friendly tool for recognizing symptoms of depression through a series of carefully crafted questions.


What We Offer

We offer a set of evidence-based questions, powered by a machine learning model, that are designed to evaluate common symptoms of depression. While we do not replace professional diagnosis or treatment, our platform acts as a preliminary guide, encouraging individuals to reflect on their emotional health and seek the support they need.


Our Mission

Our mission is to empower people by providing a simple yet effective method to assess their mental health. We aim to bridge the gap between unawareness and action, helping individuals take that crucial first step toward seeking professional help and prioritizing their mental wellness.
